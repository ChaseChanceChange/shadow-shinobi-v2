# Shadow Shinobi Economy & Summoning Rules

## Design target

Shadow Shinobi can make money without selling combat power directly. The fantasy is a difficult persistent squad RPG where reaching the highest tiers takes time, planning, losses and resource discipline.

## The line we do not cross

Paid purchases must never directly grant an exclusive combat advantage that a free player cannot eventually earn through normal play.

No paid-only operative with higher base stats.
No paid-only weapon with higher maximum power.
No paid-only talent node.
No paid-only resource that is required to finish endgame progression.
No direct purchase of PvP power.

## Where the gacha-like layer lives

We can use a controlled **Vault / Relic Draw** system for discovery, cosmetics, alternate visual identities and side-grade collection pieces.

The draw currency should have three sources:

1. Earned from contracts, world events, achievements and seasonal play.
2. A small amount from optional premium purchases.
3. Free login / progression grants.

Premium purchases can accelerate attempts or provide cosmetic bundles, but the combat outcome must remain achievable without payment.

## Pity and protection

Every limited draw has a visible pity counter.

- Hard pity guarantees the featured item by the configured threshold.
- Duplicate high-rarity combat pieces convert into universal fragments.
- Universal fragments can be saved toward a player-selected item.
- No hidden pity reset on duplicate drops.
- The UI always shows odds and the remaining pity distance.

## Resource pressure

The game should deliberately cap some farming loops so the player cannot brute-force the entire progression tree in one weekend.

Primary limits:

- Contract energy / resolve.
- Daily high-value relic expeditions.
- Weekly apex hunts.
- Forge catalyst availability.
- Limited world-event windows.

Ordinary low-value play remains available. The limit protects the economy rather than blocking the game.

## God-tier progression

The final progression band is intentionally long.

Power should come from several axes rather than one spend button:

- operative level and mastery
- squad synergy
- gear quality
- gear enhancement
- rare individualized substats
- weapon talent depth
- relic discoveries
- faction/world unlocks
- player knowledge

The strongest builds therefore require time and successful execution, not a purchase receipt.

## Monetisation candidates

Safe candidates are:

- cosmetic weapon skins
- alternate operative appearances
- profile banners / frames
- emotes
- camp / command-room cosmetics
- extra loadout slots
- convenience purchases that do not bypass hard progression
- optional premium currency that still respects the same pity rules

## Combat presentation principle

Resources create the tension; combat creates the payoff. A player should feel the impact of a rare build through animation, timing, hit effects, turn-order pressure, critical moments and meaningful finishing attacks.
