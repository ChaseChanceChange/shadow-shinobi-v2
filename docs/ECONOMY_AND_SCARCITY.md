# Shadow Shinobi — Economy & Scarcity

The economy is designed to make progression meaningful without selling combat dominance.

## Resource pressure

Players should never be able to farm every resource infinitely from one optimal route. World activities rotate, nodes recover, dangerous zones carry higher risk, and major events create temporary opportunities.

Core pressure resources:

- Coins: general progression and services.
- Gear Fragments: forging and recovery material.
- Energy: limits repeated world actions during a session.
- Relic knowledge: discoverable progression that opens new possibilities.

## God-tier chase

The strongest builds require investment, risk, specialization and time. A player can reach the highest practical combat tier through gameplay. Optional purchases should never be required to defeat a player who has invested heavily in a different build.

## Gacha/Vault direction

A future collection/Vault layer may use randomized acquisition for cosmetics, alternate weapon appearances, convenience, and collection targets. Direct combat power must remain bounded by gameplay systems and deterministic alternatives.

A pity/guarantee layer should prevent an unlimited negative-value loop. Duplicate results should convert into useful universal progression material rather than dead inventory.

## Legendary philosophy

Legendary weapons are sidegrades. Their prestige comes from history, branching mechanics, visual evolution and world consequences rather than raw stat inflation.

## Scarcity rules

- High-value world resources should have meaningful travel or combat risk.
- Major relic fragments are tradeable and form a player-driven market.
- Rare opportunities rotate through the world instead of remaining permanently farmable in one spot.
- Maxed legendary items are intentionally difficult to replace.
- Losing a legendary can generate new world content rather than deleting its story.

## Design test

A paying player may save time, acquire cosmetics, or participate in collections more conveniently. They should not be able to buy an outcome that a non-paying player cannot eventually achieve through play.
